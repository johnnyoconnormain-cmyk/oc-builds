Breakfast at 7, last call at 9. Homemade everything, local ingredients, and a patio that faces the mountains.

A family restaurant in downtown Ellensburg.

Lisa and Tran Nguyen opened Valley Grille in 2011. The menu changes with the seasons, but the biscuits are always the same recipe — Lisa's grandmother's, from a handwritten card taped inside the pantry door.

Started as a breakfast spot with 8 tables.

Lisa spent 15 years managing restaurants in Seattle before deciding she was done cooking for other people's bottom line. They moved to Ellensburg because Tran grew up on a farm outside Thorp.

Word got around. By 2014 they'd expanded into dinner service, added the patio in 2018, and their daughter Mai joined as sous chef after culinary school in Portland.

We source from Kittitas Valley farms when we can: Kittitas Meats, Iron Horse Bakery, Thorp fruit stands in summer.
