Started with one chair and a handshake.

Miguel Ramirez Sr. opened El Rancho in '87 with one barber chair he bought from a shop closing down in Yakima. The name came from his grandfather's ranch in Jalisco — a place where everyone was welcome and nobody was in a rush.

Now his son Miguel Jr. runs the shop with two other barbers. The chair's still there, third one from the door. The philosophy hasn't changed either.

We cut hair for college kids, ranchers, businessmen, and the same old-timers who've been coming since the Reagan years. If you've been here once, you're family.

Miguel Jr. behind the chair, 2024
