Our story

When the last Planet Fitness closed in 2015, the nearest gym was in Yakima. That didn't sit right with Jake Hensley.

He signed a lease on the old feed store building off Canyon Road, bought used equipment from a gym closing in Moses Lake, and opened the doors in January 2016. First month: 38 members.

Still the same building. Still no smoothie bar. Still no contracts.

— Jake Hensley, Owner

What we've got

Free Weights

Dumbbells to 120lbs, Olympic barbells, bumper plates, kettlebells 15-100lbs.

Power Racks

Four squat racks with safety bars. Dedicated bench station.

Cardio

Treadmills, bikes, rowers, and a ski erg that nobody uses but Jake.

Cables & Machines

Dual cable, lat pulldown, leg press, hack squat.

Functional Area

Turf strip, battle ropes, plyo boxes, sleds. Heavy bag in the corner.

Locker Rooms

Clean showers, day-use lockers, water bottle fill station.
